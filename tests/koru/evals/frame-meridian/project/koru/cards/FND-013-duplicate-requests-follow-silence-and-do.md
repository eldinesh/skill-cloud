# FND-013 · Duplicate requests follow silence and double clicks

## Observation

P03 reports about a third of 60 to 80 overnight requests are duplicates: the same unit asking for the same item twice after hearing nothing. Ticket #4490 (North Maternity) describes clicking submit, seeing nothing happen, clicking again and creating two requests. Two different patterns: re-asking after silence, and double submission. The Q3 duplicate rate is FND-024. Engineering prefers flag-and-merge to automatic blocking (FND call, DAT-009).

## Quotes

> "Maybe a third are duplicates, the same unit asking for the same thing twice because they didn't hear back." (P03, DAT-003)
