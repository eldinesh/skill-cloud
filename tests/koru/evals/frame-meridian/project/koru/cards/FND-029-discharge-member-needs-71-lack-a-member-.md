# FND-029 · Discharge member needs: 71% lack a member ID, 24% were raised more than once, 18% after discharge

## Observation

Q3 recorded 689 discharge member needs in UnitDesk (388 North, 301 Central). 71% had no plan member ID, 24% were raised more than once, and 18% were created after the patient had already left. Needs sent by email or phone are not in these counts.
