# FND-022 · Supplies: 3% need approval and the median approval is 14 minutes; equipment: 62% and 95 minutes

## Observation

Q3, both sites: 3% of supplies requests were over 500 USD and the median time from submitted to approved was 14 minutes (the figure includes auto-approval under 500 USD). For equipment, 62% were over 500 USD and the median was 95 minutes.
