# FND-026 · Median nurse time on requests is 18 minutes a shift and the 90th percentile is 52; phone time was not measured

## Observation

A Q3 time-and-motion audit of 40 shifts measured a median of 18 minutes per shift on request admin and a 90th percentile of 52 minutes. Time on the phone away from the unit was not included. The audit does not say which units or roles were observed. The 40-minute figure is a stakeholder estimate (FND-033). A survey of 212 respondents scored UnitDesk 2.1 out of 5 (DAT-005, not made a separate Finding).
