# IDE-008 · Look and feel of a consumer app

## Idea

The new screens use Meridian UI and "should look modern, like a consumer app" (PRD).

## Reverse trace

No user problem is named for the look itself; nobody interviewed asked for it. What the evidence does show is that the current library and screens fall short: the tablet layout fails (FND-019), primary blue fails small-text contrast and spacing and type are hard-coded (FND-040), and seven needed components are missing (FND-039). That is foundation work (PRB-005), and it is a different thing from a consumer look. Held as an idea.
