# CON-006 · Phase 1 scope: which request types, which sites, and the plan side

## Positions

- **A. Everything: all request types, all four hospitals, plan if possible.** Brief ("one requests platform", four hospitals); PRD goals and R1, R10 (all four hospitals) against section 8 (phase 1: North and Central). Document; the brief and the PRD already disagree on sites.
- **B. Supplies first; the plan side is phase 2.** Dana at kick-off ("it's phase 2. I don't want the plan side slowing down the supply work").
- **C. The plan side belongs in phase 1.** Hannah at kick-off ("The supply work isn't where members get hurt"). Backed by member complaints, one with a readmission (FND-020), P04 saying things go wrong every week (FND-017) and 71% of discharge needs without a member ID (FND-029).
- **D. One request type done properly in eight weeks.** Tom Okafor: supplies, with suggestions, duplicate flags, one-click approval and clear status; if the plan side comes in, "cut something else". Engineer's capacity estimate.

## Weighing

This is not a dispute about facts; the sources measure different things. Supplies are 80.5% of volume (FND-021); discharge needs are 3.1% of volume and carry the harm (FND-020, FND-017, FND-029). Neither claim is wrong: volume and harm point to different phases. Averaging them gives a release that does neither well, and the engineer's estimate says it cannot do both.

Weak spots to keep in view: the harm evidence is one navigator, two complaints (probably chosen for severity) and a possible single event told three times (FND-017). The counts come from UnitDesk only, so phone and email discharge needs are absent from the 689. Equipment is 11% of volume with 62% over 500 USD and a 22-hour median delivery, and nobody has made a case for or against it in phase 1.

A decision is needed from the person who can make it. Options for a Decision card: (1) supplies only, with status; (2) supplies plus a thin discharge handoff slice (one intake, plan member ID, confirmation back to the unit); (3) discharge first. Each carries a named cost.

Proposed for the person to set: **taken to the client** (Dana, with Hannah, Priya and Tom in the room).
