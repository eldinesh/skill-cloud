# IDE-005 · Native mobile app

## Idea

A native mobile app, so staff can request and track on any device.

## Reverse trace

Implies that staff, especially night and agency staff, work off the shared workstation: 34% of sessions come from a phone browser and 27% of North night sessions use the shared login (FND-031), and staff text the charge nurse from personal phones (FND-011). That bears on PRB-003 and INS-002 (medium). It does not show that an app is the answer. No native app is approved and phones are against policy (CON-007); IT policy on phone browsers goes to the client via Priya. Held as an idea; design to the workstation and tablet now.
