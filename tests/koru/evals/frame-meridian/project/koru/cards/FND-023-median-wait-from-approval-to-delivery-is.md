# FND-023 · Median wait from approval to delivery is 410 minutes for supplies and 1,320 for equipment

## Observation

Q3, both sites: the median from approved to marked delivered was 410 minutes (6 hours 50 minutes) for supplies and 1,320 minutes (22 hours) for equipment. The notes say the figure depends on the 10:00 and 16:00 delivery rounds. Supplies median approval is 14 minutes (FND-022).
