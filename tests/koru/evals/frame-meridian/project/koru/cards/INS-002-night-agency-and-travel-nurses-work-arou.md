# INS-002 · Night, agency and travel nurses work around the system, not in it, so the hardest shifts leave no record

## What

The people who need supplies most urgently, at night and from agencies, often have no login of their own and use shared accounts, texts and walking to other units.

## Why

Because the system assumes a permanent staff member at a personal workstation, anyone outside that pattern goes around it, and their need disappears from the data.

## So what

Any design must let a nurse ask under their own name from wherever they are on shift, without a directory account, or the night stockout pattern will stay invisible.
