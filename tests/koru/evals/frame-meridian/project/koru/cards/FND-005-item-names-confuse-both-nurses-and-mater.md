# FND-005 · Item names confuse both nurses and materials staff

## Observation

P01 describes picking from a dropdown of a thousand items with names such as DRSG FOAM SIL 10X10. P03 reports nurses type names such as "gloves medium" and materials staff call back to ask which of four kinds is meant. Ticket #4483 (Central Materials) lists three names received for one glove: "the blue gloves", "gloves (normal)" and "GLOVE NITR M". The catalogue holds plain names for about 1,100 of about 6,400 items (client.md, engineering note).

## Quotes

> "you have to pick from this dropdown of a thousand things with names like" (P01, DAT-001)
> "Nurses type "gloves medium" and there are four kinds of medium gloves. I call them back to ask which." (P03, DAT-003)
