# JOB-005 · Get home equipment arranged for a patient going home and know who has it

Rests on INS-003 (signed, medium confidence). The nurse side rests on one interview (P01), one ticket and the complaints.

## Job

When a patient is going home and needs equipment or a ride, I want to send the need once to the right place and see that it is arranged, so I can discharge on time and the patient goes home with what they need.

## Forces

- **Push:** "I don't even know who to send that to" (P01); UnitDesk "Equipment" against "the plan handles home equipment" (#4498); a 4-hour wait with transport not booked (MP-2307); oxygen not delivered (MP-2291, FND-017).
- **Pull:** one place to send it, and a confirmation with a date.
- **Habit:** email the plan, phone the main line, ask the social worker.
- **Anxiety:** putting a member's details in an email or in a system nobody watches; being the person who "put it in the system" when nothing happened.
