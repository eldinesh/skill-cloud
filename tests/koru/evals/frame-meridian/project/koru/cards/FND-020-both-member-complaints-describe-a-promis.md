# FND-020 · Both member complaints describe a promised arrangement that did not happen

## Observation

Both member complaints describe being told something was arranged that did not happen. MP-2291: a father sent home from North on Friday was told oxygen would be delivered; nobody came; the hospital said call the plan; the plan said it had no record; he was readmitted on Sunday. MP-2307: a member waited 4 hours for discharge with transport not booked; the nurse said at 10am the need would be "put in the system" and at 1pm that a call would be made. The two complaints were passed on by Meridian Plan and may have been chosen for severity.
