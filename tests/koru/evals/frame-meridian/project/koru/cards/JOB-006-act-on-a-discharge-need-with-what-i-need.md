# JOB-006 · Act on a discharge need with what I need and tell the nurse it is arranged

Rests on INS-003 (signed, medium confidence). One navigator interviewed (P04); Hannah's account is stakeholder say-so.

## Job

When a hospital sends me a discharge need for a plan member, I want the member, what is needed, the date they go home and where, in one place, and to mark it arranged for the nurse to see, so I can get it there before the member leaves.

## Forces

- **Push:** "Every way possible"; 71% of discharge needs have no member ID and the hospital shows its own record number; the email went to someone on leave; members call after they are home.
- **Pull:** "discharge requests for our members, with a date" and "got it, it's arranged for Tuesday" (P04); a coverage yes or no.
- **Habit:** inbox, main phone line, matching people by hand.
- **Anxiety:** seeing clinical notes ("I shouldn't"); PHI access and audit; one more system to watch.
