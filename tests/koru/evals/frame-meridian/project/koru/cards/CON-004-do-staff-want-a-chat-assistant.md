# CON-004 · Do staff want a chat assistant?

## Positions

- **A. Staff would like a chat assistant to raise and track requests.** Earlier synthesis finding 6, "medium evidence", no quote or count given (DAT-007); PRD R11 and the PRD job "As a nurse, I want an AI chatbot" (DAT-013, document, written by the product owner).
- **B. They do not.** P01, asked directly: "I don't have time to chat with a computer." Ticket #4511: "Love the idea of AI but please don't make me talk to a chatbot. I just need to find gloves." (FND-007, attitude and story, 1 interviewee and 1 ticket.)

## Weighing

Neither side is strong. B is two voices from the same site (North), one prompted; no interviewee we hold asked for chat unprompted, and P02, P03 and P04 were not asked. A has no visible evidence: the synthesis may rest on the same four interviews, whose transcripts do not contain a request for chat, and a persona-level "would like" is not behaviour.

What all four do ask for is the thing chat is meant to give: where the request is and when it is coming (FND-001, 4 of 4). So the need is clear and the chat form of it is unsupported. Chat is a solution (see the Idea cards the PRD implies) and is held until Explore's divergence is done.

Proposed for the person to set: **bet**. Explore carries one Direction organised around visible status and one that includes conversational help, tested on the same hard case with the question asked of all four roles.
