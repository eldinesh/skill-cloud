# INS-001 · Silence after submitting is the root cost: staff phone, resubmit and stash because nothing confirms a request was seen or when it will come

## What

Nurses and materials staff cannot tell whether a request was seen or when it will arrive. 22% of supply requests stayed 'Submitted' until bulk close (FND-025), and three of four interviewees describe checking by phone.

## Why

Duplicates follow silence: 31-36% of supply requests are duplicates (FND-024) and P01 and P03 tie resubmitting to not knowing (FND-013). Whether stashing has the same cause is not shown; FND-004 rests on two voices and stays a hypothesis for Explore.

## So what

Make the request visibly acknowledged and its next step and arrival window visible, and measure the duplicate rate before and after. Approval speed is not the lever for supplies (14 minutes; FND-022), whatever the Director's view (ASM-001).
