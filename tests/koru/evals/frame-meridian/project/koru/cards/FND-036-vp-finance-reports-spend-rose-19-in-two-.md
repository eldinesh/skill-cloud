# FND-036 · VP Finance reports spend rose 19% in two categories during the 2022 auto-replenishment pilot

## Observation

Raj Menon reports that spend on two categories rose 19% in three months during a 2022 vendor auto-replenishment pilot, and attributes it to over-ordering. The 19% figure is a recollection with no supporting data in the pack. P03 independently recalls a 2022 vendor auto-reorder and three months returning stock, which supports that the pilot happened and was costly to unwind, not the 19%.

## Quotes

> "When we piloted auto-replenishment with a vendor in 2022, our spend on two categories went up 19% in three months because it over-ordered "just in case"." (Raj, DAT-008)
> "We tried a vendor auto-reorder in 2022. I spent three months returning stock." (P03, DAT-003)
