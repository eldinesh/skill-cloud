# ASM-001 · Approvals are the main bottleneck in requests

## Claim

Requests over 500 USD wait for a manager who is busy on the floor, so removing or automating approvals would speed fulfilment significantly.

## How we would test it

Q3 times by type are already in (FND-022, FND-023). Still missing: medians for the 3% of supply lines over 500 USD, and for equipment split by whether the approver was on leave. Ask Operations Technology for both. See CON-001.
