# PRB-004 · A member's discharge need falls between the hospital and the plan

Rests on INS-003 (signed, medium). It is a harm case, not a volume case (3% of requests). Whether it is in phase 1 is not settled here: CON-006 goes to the client (Dana, with Hannah, Priya and Tom) at Brief co-sign.

## Who

Members being discharged from North and Central, the nurses who arrange their equipment or transport, and care navigators at Meridian Plan.

## What

Discharge equipment and transport needs reach the plan by email, phone, a forwarded UnitDesk request or the social worker. 71% of the 689 recorded in Q3 had no plan member ID, 24% were raised more than once and 18% were raised after the patient had left. No confirmation goes back to the unit. Members have gone home without oxygen and waited four hours for transport. Evidence: FND-014, FND-015, FND-016, FND-017, FND-020, FND-029.

## Wow

A discharge need goes in once, to one place, with the plan member ID attached, and the nurse sees it arranged with a date; navigators see what they need and a coverage yes or no, never clinical notes.

Measures (Q3 baseline): raised after discharge, 18%; without member ID, 71%; raised more than once, 24%; member complaints about discharge (two supplied, selected for severity). Counts cover UnitDesk only; phone and email needs are not counted.
