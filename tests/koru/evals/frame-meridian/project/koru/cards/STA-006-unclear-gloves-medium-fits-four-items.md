# STA-006 · Unclear: gloves medium fits four items

## Design

She wrote "gloves medium". Four catalogue items fit (nitrile, latex, vinyl, sterile surgical), the case P03 describes: "there are four kinds of medium gloves. I call them back to ask which" (FND-005). The AI does not pick. It says "Not sure", lists the four with their plain and catalogue names and a note on each, and says her unit asked for nitrile in 6 of its last 8 glove requests, but that it will not choose for her. No option is pre-selected and Send is disabled until she picks (the latex option is one a patient could be allergic to). After she picks, the suggestion becomes a confirmed single item with "Confirmed by you from the four matches". Open at `?state=STA-006`. Evidence for the numbers is synthetic. Whether to show the unit's history at all, as it could push her toward the common choice, is a test question.
