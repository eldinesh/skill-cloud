# FND-006 · Interviewees want AI to suggest and show its working, not to order

## Observation

Asked whether AI suggestions would help, P01 and P03 each accept a suggestion when the reason is shown and a person decides, and each reject AI ordering on its own. P03 recalls a 2022 vendor auto-reorder that took three months of returns. Prompted attitude: the interviewer's question named suggestion as the premise. P02 and P04 were not asked.

## Quotes

> "Suggest, maybe, if it's right. But I want to see why." (P01, DAT-001)
> "And I don't want it ordering on its own." (P01, DAT-001)
> "Flag it, let me decide." (P03, DAT-003)
> "If it ordered on its own I'd be cleaning up its mess." (P03, DAT-003)
