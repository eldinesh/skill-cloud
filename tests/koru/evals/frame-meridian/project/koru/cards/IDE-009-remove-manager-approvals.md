# IDE-009 · Remove manager approvals

## Idea

Remove or automate manager approvals so requests move faster.

## Reverse trace

Implies that approval is where requests lose time. For supplies the counts point elsewhere: 3% need approval, median 14 minutes, against a median 410 minutes from approval to delivery (FND-022, FND-023), and the materials manager says the slow part is working out what was meant (FND-037, one interview). Caveat: the 14 minutes includes auto-approvals under 500 USD, and over-500 supply lines are unmeasured (CON-001). Lines over 500 USD need ERP approval regardless (FND-035). For equipment, an absent approver stalled 40 requests (FND-018), but no Insight covers approvers; equipment approval is a Brief constraint, not a Problem. Held as an idea; the Brief puts removing supply approvals out of scope.
