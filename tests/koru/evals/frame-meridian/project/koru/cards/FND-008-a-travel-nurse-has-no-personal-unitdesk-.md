# FND-008 · A travel nurse has no personal UnitDesk login

## Observation

P02, a travel nurse on nights at North seven weeks into a 13-week contract, has no UnitDesk login. P02 asks the charge nurse or phones stores instead, and names a shared login taped inside the supply room door. Ticket #4480 (shared login not working at 2am, no one to call) and ticket #4519 (an agency nurse cannot tell which requests on the shared login are theirs) describe the same situation. The one travel nurse in the interview set; share of night sessions on the shared login is FND-031.

> "Usually I ask the charge nurse, or I just call stores." (P02, DAT-002)
