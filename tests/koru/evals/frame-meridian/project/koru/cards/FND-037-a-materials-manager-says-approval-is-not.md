# FND-037 · A materials manager says approval is not the slow step for supplies

## Observation

Asked about approvals over 500 USD, P03 says most of those are equipment, that supplies are cheap per line, and that the slow part is working out what units want and then waiting for the twice-daily delivery round. One participant, the one with the materials view. Analytics on approval and delivery times are separate findings (FND-022, FND-023) and the earlier synthesis says the opposite (DAT-007).

## Quotes

> "Most of those are equipment, not supplies. Supplies are cheap per line. The approval isn't the slow bit." (P03, DAT-003)
> "The slow bit is me working out what they actually want, and then the delivery round, which is twice a day." (P03, DAT-003)
