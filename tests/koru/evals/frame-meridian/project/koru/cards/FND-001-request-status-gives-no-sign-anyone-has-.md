# FND-001 · Request status gives no sign anyone has seen it

## Observation

All four interviewees describe not knowing whether a request has been seen, or when it will arrive. P01 reports the status reads only "Submitted" and phones materials as well. P02 asks to be told whether anyone is bringing what was requested. P03 reports units cannot see when their request is on a round. P04 asks to mark a discharge need as arranged, with a date, for the nurse to see. Ticket #4475 (Central ICU) reports a request showing Submitted since Tuesday; ticket #4519 (North nights) reports an agency nurse who cannot tell whether IV kits asked for at 1am are coming. The analytics count of requests left at Submitted until month end is FND-025.

Note: P04's version is the reverse direction (navigator to nurse), so the count is 4 of 4 for the gap but 3 of 4 for "I cannot see my own request".

## Quotes

> "It just says "Submitted". It said "Submitted" for a request I made in August." (P01, DAT-001)
> "tell me if anyone's actually bringing it" (P02, DAT-002)
> "units can see when their stuff is on the next round" (P03, DAT-003)
> "be able to say "got it, it's arranged for Tuesday" so the nurse knows." (P04, DAT-004)
