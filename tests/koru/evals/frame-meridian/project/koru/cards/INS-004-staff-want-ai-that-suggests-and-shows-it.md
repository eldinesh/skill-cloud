# INS-004 · Two interviewees asked about AI wanted it to suggest and show its working; nobody asked for it to order or to talk

## What

P01 and P03, the two interviewees asked about AI (both with 'suggest' in the question), wanted it to propose item and quantity and say why. The one person asked about a chat assistant declined it.

## Why

Both tie this to carrying the consequences of a wrong order. The 2022 pilot's spend rise is a stakeholder recollection (FND-036), recorded as context, not evidence.

## So what

Test AI as a suggestion with its reason and an undo inside the request flow against the client's ordering idea. Neither ordering nor chat is ruled out by evidence; both are bets.
