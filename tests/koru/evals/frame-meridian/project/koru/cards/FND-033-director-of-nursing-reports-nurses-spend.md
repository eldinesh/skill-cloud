# FND-033 · Director of Nursing reports nurses spend 40 minutes a shift on requests

## Observation

Marcus Lee reports that nurses tell him they spend 40 minutes a shift on requests: finding the form, calling materials, calling again. It is relayed, not measured. The Q3 audit median is 18 minutes with a 90th percentile of 52, and excludes phone time (FND-026). The earlier synthesis (DAT-007) repeats the 40 minutes as "strong evidence, confirmed by the Director of Nursing".
