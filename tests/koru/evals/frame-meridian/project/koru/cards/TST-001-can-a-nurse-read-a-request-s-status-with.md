# TST-001 · Can a nurse read a request's status without phoning

## Scenario

Tests risk R1 in koru/test-plan-explore.md. Prototype: request-tracker (default screen).

**Read aloud:** "It is 14:20. Earlier today you asked for gloves and you want to know where that stands. Without calling anyone, tell me whether anyone has looked at it, what happens next and roughly when it will be with you."

**Complete when** she names all three, correct to the screen, without a prompt: who saw it and when, the next step, the arrival. A missing or wrong part is recorded as which part.

**Measures to put a number on (Lena saves the string with the DIR threshold):** parts correct unaided out of 3 · says she would phone, yes or no, in her words · assists · time · SEQ.
