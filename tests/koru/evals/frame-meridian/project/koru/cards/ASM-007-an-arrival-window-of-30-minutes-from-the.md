# ASM-007 · An arrival window of 30 minutes from the start of the round is something stores can keep

## Claim

Both prototypes show an arrival window as the round time to 30 minutes after it (16:00 to 16:30). The one observation we hold is a delivery at 16:10 on the 16:00 round (ticket #4522, FND-012). The 30 minutes is a design choice, not a measured figure.

## How we would test it

Ask the materials manager and one stores porter how late a round runs in practice and whether they would stand behind a window. In sessions, watch whether a missed window costs more trust than a single time. If stores cannot keep it, show the round time alone.
