# ASM-005 · A leadership dashboard will lead to decisions

## Claim

A live view of where requests are stuck will let leadership act (brief; PRD R10). Asked what decision it would enable, Dana answered "Call someone, I suppose. I just want to know."

## How we would test it

Ask Dana, Raj and Marcus for the three decisions they would make from the view and who they would call; check the data exists: status depends on materials managers updating by hand, and 22% of supply requests were never updated (FND-025). Owner of the dashboard is an open question in the PRD.
