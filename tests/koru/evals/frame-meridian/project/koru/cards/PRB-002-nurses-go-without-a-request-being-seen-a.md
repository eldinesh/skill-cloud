# PRB-002 · Nurses go without a request being seen, and people phone, resubmit and stash

Rests on INS-001 (signed, medium confidence). INS-001 holds the causal link only for duplicates (they follow silence); that phoning and stashing share the cause is not shown, and is tested in Explore. Per INS-001, the lever is silence after submitting, not approval speed: supply approval takes a median 14 minutes, the wait after approval 410 (CON-001). Equipment approval is not part of this Problem: it is carried as a phase 1 constraint in the Brief, because no Insight covers approvers.

The Director of Nursing confirmed that approvals cost each nurse about an hour a shift (ASM-001), which proves the size of the problem.

## Who

Staff who raise supply requests at North and Central (nurses, charge nurses), and the materials staff who receive them.

## What

After a supply request is submitted, nothing tells the requester whether it was seen or which delivery round it is on. Duplicates follow (INS-001). People also phone and keep extra stock in cupboards; those two rest on self-report and are not yet tied to silence. Evidence: FND-001, FND-002, FND-012, FND-013, FND-023, FND-025, FND-030, FND-043 (the mock is a pack illustration, so it is context only).

## Wow

A requester sees on the request that it was seen and which round it is on, without phoning; a materials manager works from one list with duplicates grouped and item names mapped.

Measures (Q3 baseline): share of supply requests left at Submitted until month end, 22% (FND-025); duplicate rate, 36% North and 31% Central (FND-024); calls to stores, 10,970, which are not linked to requests today so a link is needed to measure them (FND-030). Do not use the 40 minutes a shift as a baseline until measured (CON-002).
