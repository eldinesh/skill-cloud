# JOB-003 · Decide what to fulfil from one clean list

Rests on INS-001 (signed, medium) for the duplicates (over-requesting as a workaround for silence is not shown), and INS-004 (signed, low; two prompted interviewees) for the suggest-and-show part. One materials manager interviewed (P03), so the AI half of this job is a low-confidence bet. Item-name confusion (FND-005) is not covered by any Insight; it stays here as Finding-level evidence.

## Job

When overnight requests arrive, I want one list with duplicates grouped, real item names, and what each unit normally uses, so I can decide what to fulfil and put on the next round without calling units back.

## Forces

- **Push:** 60 to 80 overnight requests, about a third duplicates; "gloves medium" has four kinds; units ask for 50 when they need 10; last night's ERP count and a walk to the stockroom; calls not in the system.
- **Pull:** "this unit normally uses 12 a week, they've asked for 50, that would be gold"; "Flag it, let me decide" (P03).
- **Habit:** checking the stockroom by hand; phoning back to ask which item.
- **Anxiety:** an AI that orders by itself ("I'd be cleaning up its mess", the 2022 pilot); a wrong stock number; a suggestion with no reason shown.
