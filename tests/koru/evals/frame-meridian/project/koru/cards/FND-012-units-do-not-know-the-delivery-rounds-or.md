# FND-012 · Units do not know the delivery rounds or the cut-off

## Observation

P03 reports that a request approved at 4:05 is delivered the next morning and that nobody on the units knows this. Ticket #4522 (Central Med-Surg) reports a delivery at 16:10 for a request approved at 16:02, and asks why it missed the round. Rounds are at 10:00 and 16:00, with a cut-off 30 minutes before each (client.md, engineering note). The Q3 approval-to-delivery medians are FND-023.

## Quotes

> "If your request is approved at 4:05, you get it tomorrow morning. Nobody on the units knows that." (P03, DAT-003)
