# TST-004 · Can a nurse send a right suggestion in one tap and say where it is

## Scenario

Tests risks R1 and R5 in koru/test-plan-explore.md. Prototype: ask-and-suggest (default screen).

**Read aloud:** "You need a foam dressing for a patient's wound. Ask for it however feels natural, then tell me where the request is and when it will come."

**Complete when** she sends one box of the foam dressing with one tap after the suggestion, then states seen, next step and arrival correctly from the sent screen.

**Measures:** taps from the first screen to sent · time · read the reasons, yes or no (asked at task end: "why did it choose that?") · assists · SEQ.
