# FND-004 · Units keep extra stock and request more than they need

## Observation

P01 says every unit keeps a stash of extra stock and that the next unit then runs out. P03 gives an example of units requesting 50 when they need 10, and says stock expires in unit cupboards. Two of four interviewees, in two roles and two sites. P02 describes borrowing, not stashing. The Q3 write-off figure is FND-032; the September cupboard audit is FND-028.

## Quotes

> "We keep a stash. Everybody does." (P01, DAT-001)
> "Then of course the next unit runs out." (P01, DAT-001)
> "Units ask for 50 when they need 10, because they're scared of running out. Then stuff expires in their cupboards." (P03, DAT-003)
