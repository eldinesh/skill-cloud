# IDE-001 · AI chatbot for staff (PRD R11)

## Idea

An AI chatbot that answers questions about request status (PRD R11), and lets a nurse raise and track requests by chat.

## Reverse trace

Implies the Problem that a requester cannot find out where a request is (PRB-002, INS-001). The research does not show that staff want a chat to do it: one participant was asked and declined (FND-007), P02, P03 and P04 were not asked, and the earlier synthesis claim has no visible evidence (CON-004). Held as an idea; Explore tests it against visible status.
