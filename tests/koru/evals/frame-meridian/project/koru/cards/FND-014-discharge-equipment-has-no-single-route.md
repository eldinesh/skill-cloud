# FND-014 · Discharge equipment has no single route

## Observation

P01 does not know where to send discharge equipment and lists UnitDesk, emailing the plan, and the social worker. P04 receives discharge needs by email from a nurse, a forwarded UnitDesk request, a call to the main line, a social worker message, and sometimes a call from the member after going home. Ticket #4498 (Central Orthopaedics, High) asks where to send a walker and raised toilet seat request: UnitDesk offers "Equipment" and the plan said it handles home equipment. The Q3 discharge counts are FND-029.

## Quotes

> "I don't even know who to send that to. Sometimes UnitDesk, sometimes I email the plan, sometimes the social worker does it. Nobody knows." (P01, DAT-001)
> "Every way possible." (P04, DAT-004)
> "Email from a nurse, a UnitDesk request that someone forwards to us, a phone call to our main line, a message from the hospital social worker." (P04, DAT-004)
