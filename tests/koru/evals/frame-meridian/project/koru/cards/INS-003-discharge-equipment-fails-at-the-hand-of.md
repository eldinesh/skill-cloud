# INS-003 · Discharge equipment fails at the hand-off: no single route, no member ID, and no one who says it is arranged

## What

When a patient needs equipment at home, the hospital and the health plan each assume the other has it. The plan's navigator gets requests without a member ID, often twice, and sometimes after discharge.

## Why

Because there is no single route and no named owner for a discharge need, a missing fact or an absent approver stops it silently, and the member finds out at home.

## So what

Give a discharge need one way in with the member ID attached, one owner, and a confirmation back to the nurse with a date. This is small in volume (3%) but carries the harm.
