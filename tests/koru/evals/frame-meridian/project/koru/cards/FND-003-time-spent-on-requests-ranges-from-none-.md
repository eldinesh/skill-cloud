# FND-003 · Time spent on requests ranges from none to an hour a shift

## Observation

P01 says time spent on requests varies by day, from none to an hour, and places it in chasing (calling, waiting on hold, walking to stores) rather than in filling the form. One participant, one unit type. Compare the audit median in FND-026 and the 40-minute claim in FND-033.

## Quotes

> "Some days nothing. Some days an hour. It's not the form, it's the chasing." (P01, DAT-001)
> "Calling, waiting on hold, walking down to stores." (P01, DAT-001)
