# FND-021 · Supplies are 80% of Q3 requests; equipment, transport and discharge needs are 20%

## Observation

Q3 requests created in UnitDesk at North and Central: supplies 18,055 (80.5%), equipment 2,525 (11.3%), transport 1,152 (5.1%), discharge member needs 689 (3.1%); total 22,421. Phone and email requests are not included, and the earlier synthesis, the PRD and the brief treat all of them as one "request".
