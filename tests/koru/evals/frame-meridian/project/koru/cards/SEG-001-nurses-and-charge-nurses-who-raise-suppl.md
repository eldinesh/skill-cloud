# SEG-001 · Nurses and charge nurses who raise supply requests at North and Central

## Who

Staff nurses and charge nurses at Meridian North and Central who raise supply requests on a shared ward workstation (Windows, Chrome, 1366 × 768) or a unit iPad. This is the recruiting description for the Explore tests; it is not a persona. Agency and travel nurses are a separate group (INS-002) and are not in round 1.

## Evidence

P01 (charge nurse, North) and P02 (travel nurse, nights, North) in DAT-001 and DAT-002; FND-001 (all four interviewees say a request shows no sign anyone has seen it), FND-005, FND-009, FND-013.

## Goals and jobs

JOB-001 (know it was seen and when it arrives), JOB-002 (ask under my own name at any hour).

## Differs by

Shift (nights have one person on stores), how long they have used UnitDesk, and whether they have a login of their own. Round 1 recruits at least two night-shift nurses and one nurse under a year on the tool.
