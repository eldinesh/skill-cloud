# FND-002 · Staff phone stores or materials after submitting a request

## Observation

P01 submitted a request in UnitDesk and phoned materials as well. P02 phones stores at night, where one person is on duty, and calls repeatedly. P03 reports that phone calls reach materials and are not recorded in UnitDesk. They phone because they do not trust UnitDesk, which leads to duplicate work. Three of four interviewees, in three different roles. The Q3 count of logged calls to stores is FND-030.

## Quotes

> "I called materials anyway, because if you don't call, nothing happens." (P01, DAT-001)
> "At night stores is one person, so you call and call." (P02, DAT-002)
> "Then there are the phone calls, which aren't in the system at all." (P03, DAT-003)
