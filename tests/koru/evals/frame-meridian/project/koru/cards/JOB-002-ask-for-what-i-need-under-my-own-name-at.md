# JOB-002 · Ask for what I need under my own name, at any hour

Rests on INS-002 (signed, medium confidence). Thin segment: one travel nurse interviewed; the system counts are stronger than the interviews.

## Job

When something runs out on a night shift and I am agency or travel staff without an account, I want to ask for it under my own name and see whether it is coming, so I can stay with my patients instead of borrowing and walking to another floor.

## Forces

- **Push:** no personal login; the shared login logs out every ten minutes (P02); stores is one person at night; IV start kits out at 3am and two floors walked; 27% of North night sessions on the shared login (FND-031).
- **Pull:** "Let me ask for stuff myself, and tell me if anyone's actually bringing it" (P02); see which unit holds the item (#4507; 54% of audited stockouts were for items held on the same site, FND-028).
- **Habit:** ask the charge nurse, text, call stores, borrow from the ED.
- **Anxiety:** a new account on a 13-week contract is effort; "Who would I tell? It's not my hospital"; security wants individual, time-limited accounts and an audit trail.
