# ASM-002 · Nurses lose about 40 minutes a shift to requests

## Claim

Nurses lose about 40 minutes per shift to request admin: finding the form, calling materials, calling again. Relayed by the Director of Nursing; repeated as "strong evidence" in the earlier synthesis and as an estimate in the PRD.

## How we would test it

The Q3 audit gives a median of 18 and a 90th percentile of 52 minutes, without phone time (FND-026). Shadow or diary a sample of shifts with phone and walking time included, across roles and nights. Until then 40 is not a baseline for a metric. See CON-002.
