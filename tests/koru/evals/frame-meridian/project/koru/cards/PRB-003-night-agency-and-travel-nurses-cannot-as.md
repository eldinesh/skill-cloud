# PRB-003 · Night, agency and travel nurses cannot ask for what they need by name

Rests on INS-002 (signed, medium). Thin in interviews (one travel nurse); stronger in system counts. INS-002 notes that sessions also end mid-task for permanent staff (FND-009), so the timeout is a wider problem than agency staff.

## Who

Night-shift, agency and travel nurses at North, about 20% of night shift there (Tom, DAT-009), and the charge nurse and the one person on stores who answer them.

## What

They have no individual login. The shared unit login logs out every ten minutes. At night stores is one person, and shortages are met by borrowing from other units and walking, unlogged. 27% of North night sessions use the shared login; 71% of recorded stockouts fall at nights or weekends; 54% of audited stockouts were for items another unit held. Evidence: FND-008, FND-009, FND-010, FND-027, FND-028, FND-031.

## Wow

Any nurse on shift can ask for an item under their own name in seconds at 3am, see that it is coming, and see which nearby unit holds it; borrowing is on the record.

Measures (Q3 baseline): shared-login share of North night sessions, 27%; stockout events at nights and weekends, 71% of 75 recorded (scanning units only); time to first response at night, not measured today.
