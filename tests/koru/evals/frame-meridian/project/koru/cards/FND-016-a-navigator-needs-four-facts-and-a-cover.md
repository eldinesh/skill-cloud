# FND-016 · A navigator needs four facts and a coverage yes or no, not clinical notes

## Observation

P04 lists what is needed to act on a discharge need: member ID, what is needed, going-home date and address. P04 cannot and should not see the clinical reason, and needs a yes or no that a clinician ordered it for coverage. P04 wants to see discharge requests for the plan's members, with a date, and to mark them arranged. P04 does not need supply requests. One participant; the access rule matches the PHI constraint in client.md.

## Quotes

> "No, and I shouldn't. But I need to know it's ordered by a clinician for coverage. That's a yes or no, not the notes." (P04, DAT-004)
> "I'd want to see discharge requests for our members, with a date," (P04, DAT-004)
> "I don't need the gloves." (P04, DAT-004)
