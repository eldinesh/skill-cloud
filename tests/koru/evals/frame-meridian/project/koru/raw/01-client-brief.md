# Brief from Meridian Health

**From:** Dana Whitfield, Chief Operating Officer, Meridian Health
**To:** Koru
**Subject:** AI-powered requests platform

Meridian Health runs four hospitals and Meridian Plan, a health plan covering about 180,000 members. Our frontline teams lose hours every week chasing requests: supplies that run out, equipment that doesn't turn up, and member needs at discharge that fall between the hospital and the plan.

Today, requests go through UnitDesk (our internal tool, built in 2017), phone calls, emails and a few spreadsheets. Nobody can see where anything is.

We want one requests platform, powered by AI, that:

- lets any staff member raise a request in seconds, on any device
- uses AI to predict what units will need and order it automatically, so we stop running out
- routes every request to the right team without anyone having to chase it
- gives leadership a live view of where requests are stuck

Our board has asked for an AI story in operations this year, and this is it. We'd like to see a first release in eight weeks, starting with the two largest hospitals, Meridian North and Meridian Central.

Success for us is simple: no more stockouts, and nurses back at the bedside.

We'll share our PRD, data and access to our team. Looking forward to working together.

Dana
