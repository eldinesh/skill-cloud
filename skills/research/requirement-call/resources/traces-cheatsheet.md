# TRACES cheatsheet

Ask each letter twice: about the **request** (what they want built) and the **workflow** (how the work happens today).

| Letter | Fresh start (discover) | Informed (confirm) | Ad hoc (10 min, in this order) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T** Trigger | What happened that made this come up now? | We heard [trigger] drove this. Still true? | 1. What's happening that made you call today? |
| **R** Result | What would change if this works? How will we know? | Research points to [result]. Is that the target? | 2. What does a good outcome look like for you? |
| **A** Audience | Who does this work, where, on what, and who signs off? | We studied [users]. Anyone we missed? | 4. Who's affected, and where do they work? |
| **C** Confidence | What do we know for sure, and what's assumed? | Here's what's confirmed vs assumed. Agree? | 6. What's known, and what's still a hunch? |
| **E** Edges | What can't change? What's been tried before? | Known constraints are [X]. Anything new? | 5. Anything that can't change, or already tried? |
| **S** Say-so | Who decides, how do we sync, and by when? | Who signs off, and what's the next checkpoint? | 3. Who else needs to weigh in on this? |

**Designer extras:** the constraints (tech stack, regulation, brand) · the existing product or design system (can we see it, who owns it) · what has already been tried, and why it didn't stick.

**Workflow versions** (append to any letter): "Walk me through the last time that happened." · "Who touched it before and after you?" · "What do you do when the system is slow or wrong?"

## Phrasing rules

- Past behaviour over hypotheticals: "the last time…", never "would you…".
- One question at a time. No double-barrels.
- Never pitch the solution inside the question ("wouldn't a dashboard help?").
- Silence is a tool. Count to five before rescuing.
- Playback before closing: "So what I heard is… Did I miss anything?"
