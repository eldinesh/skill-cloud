## Intro + consent (3–5 min) — read verbatim

> Thanks for making time. I'm <first name>, a researcher working with <client codename / sponsor per engagement>. We're trying to understand how <workflow> really works — there are no right answers, and nothing you say affects your job. You can skip anything or stop at any time.
>
> I'd like to <record audio / take notes>. <Tier-matched sentence — e.g. limited tier: "Recordings are transcribed, stripped of names and identifiers, and only the de-identified notes are analyzed, including with AI tools.">. Your name is never attached to quotes — reports use codes like "Participant 3". Is that okay?
>
> One ask: please don't share patient names or details. If one comes up, I may steer us back, and it won't be in our notes. Any questions before we start?

**If recording declined:** "No problem — notes only." (Mark session notes `notes-only`.)

## Warm-up (3–5 min) [MUST]
- Walk me through yesterday's shift, start to end — just the headlines.
  - ↳ Where does <target activity> show up in that day?
- How long have you been in this role / on this unit?

## §A — <RQ1 topic> (… min) [MUST]
- Tell me about the last time <target activity> happened. Start from what triggered it.
  - ↳ What did you do first? What happened next?
  - ↳ Who else was involved? What did you need from them?
- What did you have to work around that time?
  - ↳ What do you mean by <their word>?

## §B — <RQ2/RQ3 topic> (… min) [SHOULD]
- <Main question — behavior, past tense, specific instance>
  - ↳ <probe>
  - ↳ Why was that important to you?

## §C — <RQ3/RQ4 topic> (… min) [COULD]
- <Main question>
  - ↳ What would have happened if you'd done nothing?

## Wrap (3–5 min) [MUST]
- What didn't I ask about that you think matters here?
- Anything you'd like to ask me?
- Thanks + next steps: <one sentence>.

## 25–30 minute clinical cut
Intro/consent → Warm-up (one question) → §A in full → Wrap. Skip §B/§C; note the gap in session notes.

## Facilitator notes
- Probes that always work: "Tell me more about that." · "What happened next?" · 5 seconds of silence.
- Mirror their vocabulary; never introduce interface or glossary terms first.
- Interruptions (pages, call-outs): note what interrupted and how they resumed — then offer to pause or end.
- On-site: badge/escort per site rules; bare below elbows if required; never touch equipment; demos on test/training environments only — if live records appear, look away and don't capture.
- End on time, even mid-thread.
