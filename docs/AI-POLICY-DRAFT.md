# AI & Client Data Policy — DRAFT for leadership review (not yet policy)

One page. Ratify, adjust, or strike — but decide; the skills already enforce these defaults.

## The rule in one line

What we may do with client material in AI tools is set **per engagement**, by the engagement manager, from the contract — and recorded in the project's `ux/project-context.md`, where every SkillCloud skill checks it.

## The three tiers

**Prohibited** — the engagement bars AI processing of client material.
No client material in any AI tool, period. SkillCloud skills refuse and say why. AI remains available for general craft and fully synthetic work.

**Limited** — *the default for healthcare engagements unless the contract says otherwise, and the default whenever unsure.*
De-identified material only. No live PHI/PII: no names, MRNs, schedules, identifiable screenshots (staging environments count as live). De-identification happens on intake, before analysis — participant codes, codenames, Safe Harbor categories stripped.

**Permitted** — the client has approved AI tooling **in writing**.
Work normally; still de-identify people data by default.

## Always, regardless of tier

- Never paste credentials, contracts, or patient records into any AI tool.
- A stricter client NDA supersedes this policy.
- One client per project folder and session — client contexts never mix.
- Slip-ups (PHI pasted, wrong-tier processing) are reported same-day to the engagement manager. Fast honesty is policy; quiet cleanup is a violation.
